What Daybird is

Daybird reads the morning's weather forecast and recent bird sightings and tells you whether it looks worth going out, what you might see, and where. It also lets people post what they saw, lets landowners open their land to small groups on good mornings, and lets friends keep a private circle. It is a personal project run by Benjamin Morris in Lebanon, New Hampshire, offered free of charge.

Not safety advice

Daybird is a guide, not safety advice. The call (GO, MAYBE, STAY IN), the "what to wear" line, the times and the spots are opinions generated from public forecasts and reports. Forecasts are wrong sometimes and reports are incomplete. Before you go out, check the weather, the ice, the water, the road, the hunting season and the land yourself. You are responsible for your own safety and your own decisions.

eBird and other data

Sightings come from eBird, a project of the Cornell Lab of Ornithology, under eBird's API terms, which allow non-commercial use only. Daybird is free and non-commercial. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cornell. You may not use Daybird to extract eBird data for a commercial purpose. Weather comes from Open-Meteo. Wherever Daybird shows these data it says where they came from; please keep that credit if you share a brief.

Please keep the exact locations of sensitive species (owls, nesting raptors, anything eBird itself hides) vague in posts and notes.

Accounts

You sign in with an email link; there is no password. You are responsible for what happens under your account, so keep your email account secure. You must be at least 13 to have an account. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Settings; see Privacy for exactly what that removes.

What you post

Posts, photos, comments, listings and circle notes are yours. By posting them you let Daybird show them to the people the feature is for: the feed is public, listings are public except the private location, circles are visible only to members. Delete a post and it is gone from the site; copies other people made are theirs.

Be kind, report real birds, and do not post anything you do not have the right to share, including other people's photos. Daybird may remove posts or accounts that break this, and may do so without warning if the post is harmful.

Land passes

Passes connect birders with landowners. Daybird is not a party to the visit. Hosts decide who may come, when, and under what rules; guests agree to the host's rules and to the guest waiver when they book. Hosts agree to the host agreement when they list. No payment is taken through Daybird yet; if that changes, this section and the waiver will change first.

Emails

Daybird emails only when you ask it to: a brief to yourself, or one brief to a friend. The friend gets an unsubscribe link in that email and can stop all friend-sent briefs with one click. Do not use the friend feature to send briefs to people who did not ask for them.
