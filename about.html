Should you go birding this morning?

Daybird answers that in one word for the Upper Valley: GO, MAYBE, or STAY IN. Then it tells you where.

The brief

Every morning: wind, rain, last night's migration, and what's been reported nearby in the last three days, boiled down to a call and a short list of spots. Built on eBird and Open-Meteo data.

Land passes

Local landowners open a pond, a pasture, a ridge for a few birders on the mornings it's actually good. Small groups, exact location only after you book. Most listings are free or a few dollars.

The feed

What people saw today, with photos. Your profile shows your life list, your latest lifer, and your favorite bird. No algorithm, no ads.

Who it's for

Beginners who want someone to tell them where to point the binoculars, and anyone who'd rather spend three minutes reading than thirty minutes guessing. It's free to use. Sign in with your email, no password.

Hosting your land

If you own land near Lebanon, Hanover, or anywhere in the Upper Valley with water, edges, or open sky, you can list it in two minutes. You pick the days and the group size. Your address stays private until someone books. List your land →

Add it to your phone

iPhone: open this page in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome menu → Add to Home screen. It opens like an app.
