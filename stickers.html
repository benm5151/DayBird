Your birds

Print: letter, portrait, at 100% (no "fit to page"), on full-sheet sticker paper; cut around the white border. iMessage: save a sticker to Photos, then in Messages tap +, Stickers, + and pick it (or long-press the bird in Photos and choose Add Sticker). On Android, save it and send it as an image.

Wikimedia photos keep their photographer's credit on the sticker; your own photos and drawings carry none. Nothing is uploaded: the stickers are drawn in your browser.

Your list is your own eBird export. Photos via Wikimedia Commons, each credited on its sticker. About Daybird.
